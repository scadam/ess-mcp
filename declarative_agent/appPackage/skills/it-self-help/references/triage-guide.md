# IT triage guide

Use single words when searching the ServiceNow knowledge base: the search matches words in article titles.

| Symptom | Search words (try in order) | Category | Default urgency | First steps if no article fits |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Locked out, forgot password, account locked | lock, password | inquiry | 2 | Wait 15 minutes for the lockout to clear, then sign in once with the correct password. Use the self-service password reset link on the sign-in page. |
| VPN won't connect or keeps dropping | VPN, network | network | 2 | Restart the VPN client, check the internet connection works without VPN, and restart the device. |
| Wi-Fi or network not working | wireless, network | network | 2 | Turn Wi-Fi off and on, forget and rejoin the network, and try a wired connection. |
| Email or Outlook problems (not syncing, keeps asking for password, can't send) | outlook, email | software | 2 | Restart Outlook, check Outlook on the web works, and remove and re-add the account if it keeps asking for a password. |
| Laptop hardware (screen, battery, keyboard, damage) | laptop, warranty | hardware | 2 | Note the asset tag, check the warranty article, and ask whether a loaner laptop would help. |
| Computer slow or app not responding | Windows, application | software | 3 | Close unused apps, restart, and check for pending updates. |
| Phishing or suspicious email | phishing, spam | inquiry | 1 | Don't click links or open attachments. Report the message, then delete it. |
| Printer | printer | hardware | 3 | Check the printer is on the same network and restart it. |

## Urgency

- **1 — High:** the employee can't work at all and has no workaround, or several people are affected.
- **2 — Medium:** work is impaired but a workaround exists.
- **3 — Low:** minor or cosmetic; the employee can keep working.

Impact is set by the service desk; leave it blank unless the employee says many people are affected.
