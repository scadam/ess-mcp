# Slide Patterns

## Supplier Scorecard

Use for supplier performance comparison.

Columns:

- Supplier
- Tier
- Risk
- On-time rate
- Defect rate
- Average lead days
- Open orders
- Open value
- Procurement action

Narrative:

- Start with value exposure and operational risk.
- Compare strategic suppliers before approved suppliers.
- Call out medium-risk suppliers when open value is material.

## Open Order Risk Heatmap

Use for delivery and fulfilment exceptions.

Rows:

- Late
- At risk
- Partially received
- Pending supplier acknowledgement
- High value

Columns:

- PO number
- Supplier
- Category
- Department
- Expected delivery
- Open value
- Recommended action

Narrative:

- Explain the business implication of each exception.
- Link delivery risk to employee or department demand where available.

## Demand And Stock Coverage

Use for category planning.

Visual options:

- Bar chart of monthly demand by item.
- Heatmap of stock coverage by item.
- Watch-list table for stockout and low coverage items.

Narrative:

- Show where employee demand is outpacing stock.
- Connect stockout risk to upcoming ServiceNow requests and Coupa order placement.

## ServiceNow-To-Coupa Flow

Use for process visibility.

Stages:

1. ServiceNow request
2. Requested item
3. Coupa requisition
4. Coupa purchase order
5. Receipt
6. Invoice
7. Payment or exception

Narrative:

- Use one representative flow as the hero example.
- Then show aggregate counts for flows with receipts, invoices, or blockers.

## Recommended Actions

Use as the closing slide.

Columns:

- Priority
- Action
- Owner
- Supplier or category
- Evidence
- Timing

Action examples:

- Expedite overdue PO with supplier account manager.
- Confirm supplier acknowledgement for pending POs.
- Review demand plan for items below one month stock coverage.
- Resolve invoice quantity mismatch with AP and supplier.
- Schedule quarterly business review for medium-risk strategic supplier.
