# Third-party risk standard (supplier onboarding)

## Tiers
| Tier | When | Sections required |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Critical | ICT service supporting a critical or important function (DORA), or an outsourcing of a regulated activity | All five |
| 2 — High | Processes personal or client data, or annual spend over £250,000, or a high-risk jurisdiction | Information security, data protection, financial crime, financial standing |
| 3 — Standard | Everything else | Financial crime, financial standing |

Sections not required for the tier are recorded as pass, "not applicable" with the reason.

## Decision
- Approve only when every required section passes. Conditions from all reviewers carry into the contract.
- Tier 1 approvals also need an entry in the DORA register of information and notification to the regulator where
  required; list these as follow-ups.
- High-risk jurisdictions (FATF grey or black list, comprehensive sanctions) make the tier at least 2 and the
  financial crime section decisive.
