# HR exceptions policy (Caldova Group, global)

## Working temporarily from another country ("work from abroad")
| Rule | Limit | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| W1 Allowance | Up to **20 working days** in a rolling 12 months, in an approved country, with line-manager agreement | Within policy |
| W2 Exception | **21–60 working days** in 12 months | Exception panel: HR Business Partner, Tax & Global Mobility, Employment Counsel, line manager |
| W3 Maximum | Over **60 working days**, or more than 30 consecutive working days in a country with a permanent-establishment risk | Not permitted; an international assignment or transfer is the alternative |
| W4 Unapproved countries | Sanctioned countries; countries where the bank has no entity and data-export restrictions apply (for example mainland China, Russia) | Not permitted |
| W5 Regulated activity | Client-facing regulated roles may not perform regulated activities from a country where they are not licensed | Condition: internal work only |
| W6 Contracting | Sales, deal and relationship roles must not negotiate or conclude contracts while abroad (permanent-establishment risk) | Condition, and panel even within 20 days |
| W7 Client confidentiality | Roles with access to client-identifying data from Switzerland, Luxembourg, Singapore or Hong Kong booking centres may not access it from outside that jurisdiction | Condition: no client data access; panel |
| W8 Social security | Work in the EU/EEA/Switzerland needs an A1 certificate; the US and others need a certificate of coverage under a totalisation agreement where one exists | Condition |
| W9 Right to work | The employee must have the right to work in the host country (citizenship or visa); business-visitor status does not cover productive work beyond incidental activities | Condition; decline if absent |

## Leave carry-over
| Rule | Outcome |
|---|---|
| L1 | Up to **5 days** of contractual leave carried to 31 March of the next year | Within policy |
| L2 | Statutory leave the employee could not take because of sickness or family leave must be carried as the law of the country requires | Statutory right — approve |
| L3 | More than 5 days for business reasons (a project the bank asked them to stay on), with manager confirmation | Panel: HR Business Partner and line manager; up to 10 days, to be taken by 30 June |
| L4 | Anything else above 5 days | Not permitted; pay in lieu only where the country's law allows it |

## Unpaid, sabbatical and family leave
| Rule | Outcome |
|---|---|
| U1 | Unpaid leave up to 20 working days with manager agreement | Within policy |
| U2 | 21 days to 6 months (sabbatical) after 5 years' service | Panel: HR Business Partner and line manager |
| U3 | Statutory family leave (maternity, paternity, parental, carer's) | Statutory right in every country — approve and book the correct absence type |

## Records
The decision, conditions and approvers are recorded on the case. Approved leave is booked by the employee in Workday
with the absence type named in the decision; the desk confirms it appears.
