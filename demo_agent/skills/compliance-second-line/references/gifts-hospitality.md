# Gifts and hospitality (Caldova Group Code of Conduct, section 7)

All values are per person, including tax, converted to GBP at the month's rate (USD 0.79, EUR 0.86).

| Rule | Threshold / condition | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| G1 Register | Any gift or hospitality given or received above **£50** | Register entry on the case |
| G2 Line manager | Above **£100** | Line manager approval |
| G3 Compliance pre-approval | Above **£250**, or any travel or accommodation | Compliance pre-approval (this desk, within authority up to £500) |
| G4 Chief Compliance Officer | Above **£500**, or cumulative with the same counterparty above **£1,000** in 12 months | CCO approval (escalate) |
| G5 Public officials | Anything given to or received from a public official or state-owned entity employee | Not permitted without CCO approval (escalate) |
| G6 Active tender | Counterparty is bidding in an open sourcing event, or the requester approves its invoices/orders and a contract renewal falls within 90 days | Decline hospitality; gifts above £50 declined |
| G7 Cash | Cash, vouchers, gift cards, loans or securities | Always declined |
| G8 Frequency | More than **4** hospitality events from the same counterparty in 12 months | Review |
| G9 Spouse or guest | Hospitality extended to a spouse or guest | Treat as double the value; review |

Conditions commonly attached to an approval: attend with a business purpose, no travel or accommodation paid by the
counterparty, no commercial discussions about live negotiations, register within 5 working days.

Decline wording: thank the counterparty, explain the bank's policy, and offer to meet in a business setting.
