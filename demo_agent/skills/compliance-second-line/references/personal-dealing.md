# Personal account dealing (Caldova Group PAD policy)

Applies to every employee and connected persons (spouse, partner, dependent children, entities they control).

| Rule | Outcome |
|---|---|
| P1 Pre-clearance | Required for every trade in listed shares, bonds, ETFs of fewer than 20 issuers, derivatives and crypto-assets. Broad index funds and government bonds are exempt. |
| P2 Restricted list | Trades in a restricted instrument are **declined**. Never state the reason. |
| P3 Insider lists | An employee on an insider list for a project may not trade any instrument of the parties. **Declined**; never state the reason. |
| P4 Holding period | Minimum **30 days**; selling sooner needs CCO approval (escalate) unless at a loss. |
| P5 Clearance validity | Until the end of the next business day. |
| P6 Front running | No trade in an instrument the employee's desk is trading for clients, or has published research on in the last 5 days. **Declined.** |
| P7 Size | Above £100,000 notional: CCO approval (escalate). |
