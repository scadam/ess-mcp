# Working away from the office: data handling and conduct (Caldova Group Compliance standard)

Applies to every employee and contractor wherever they work: at home, while travelling, or temporarily from another
country under the HR rules. Line managers make sure their teams know it; breaches are reported to Compliance.

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| D1 Bank devices only | Bank data is handled only on bank-issued, managed devices and in approved apps (Microsoft 365, Teams, bank systems). Never on personal devices, personal email, personal cloud storage or consumer messaging apps. |
| D2 No workarounds | When the VPN or an app is unavailable, stop and contact the IT Service Desk. Forwarding bank documents to a personal address, or printing them at home, to keep working is a data breach. |
| D3 Client data from abroad | Client-identifying data may be used from another country only under an HR-approved arrangement and never from outside the Swiss, Luxembourg, Singapore or Hong Kong booking centres for clients booked there. |
| D4 Paper | No printing of client data outside a bank office. Paper that must travel goes in a locked case and is shredded in a bank office. |
| D5 Overlooking and overhearing | Work where screens can't be overlooked and calls can't be overheard; no client business in public places; use a privacy screen when travelling. |
| D6 Recorded lines | Roles that must use recorded lines (trading, sales, advisory) never discuss client orders or deals on unrecorded channels (mobile voice, WhatsApp, personal messaging), including when working remotely. |
| D7 Inside information | Deal documents don't leave approved systems without the deal team's written approval. Personal account dealing rules apply wherever you are. |
| D8 Incidents within one hour | A lost or stolen device, or bank data sent to the wrong place, is reported to the IT Service Desk and Compliance within one hour. |
| D9 Regulated activity | Advising, arranging or dealing is done only from a country where you are licensed; otherwise internal work only (HR rule W5). |
