# Conflicts of interest and outside business interests

| Rule | Outcome |
|---|---|
| C1 Declare | Any personal, family or financial interest that could influence, or appear to influence, a business decision must be declared. |
| C2 Supplier relationships | An employee with an interest in a supplier (ownership, family member employed in a decision role) must not select, approve orders or invoices for, or manage that supplier. Mitigation: remove from approval chain and record. |
| C3 Outside business interests (OBIs) | Directorships, paid roles, and advisory positions need line-manager approval and compliance review before starting. Declined if the entity is a client, competitor or supplier, or the time commitment exceeds 5 hours a week. |
| C4 Charity and public roles | Unpaid charity trustee roles are approved with conditions (no fundraising from clients). Political office needs CCO approval. |
| C5 Regulated roles | Senior managers and certified staff (SM&CR) need CCO approval for any OBI. |

Review panel for C2/C3: the line manager and the Head of Compliance Advisory. Record the mitigation on the case.
